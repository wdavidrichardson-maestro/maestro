How do I get a Client ID?
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your normal Spotify account.
  2. Click "Create app". Give it any name, like "Maestro".
  3. For "Redirect URI", paste the exact address shown above, then save.
  4. Open the app, click "Settings", and copy the "Client ID" shown there into the box above.

How do I get a Firebase config?
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with any Google account.
  2. Click "Create a project", give it any name (like "Maestro"), and you can skip/disable Google Analytics for it.
  3. Once inside the project, click the "</>" (web) icon to register a new web app. Give it a nickname and click "Register app" — you don't need hosting.
  4. Copy the whole const firebaseConfig = { ... } block shown and paste it into the box above.
  5. In the left sidebar, go to Build → Realtime Database → "Create Database" (choose any location, start in test mode).
  6. Click the "Rules" tab there and replace the contents with what I'll give you once you've reached this step — then click "Publish".
